# Support

Where to get help, and what your own people need to know. Two audiences: you in
six months, and whoever covers for you.

## Where to ask, per product

Every product here is public and takes issues on its own repository. That is the
right place for anything about the product itself — a step that did not work, a
value that could not be swapped, a prerequisite that was missing.

| Product | Issues |
|---|---|
| Campaign-site kit | [lentago/monarda](https://github.com/lentago/monarda/issues) |
| AWS platform | [lentago/solidago](https://github.com/lentago/solidago/issues) |
| Provisioning | [lentago/kalmia](https://github.com/lentago/kalmia/issues) |
| Observability | [lentago/drosera](https://github.com/lentago/drosera/issues) |
| Log capture | [lentago/betula](https://github.com/lentago/betula/issues) |
| Kubernetes platform | [lentago/osmunda](https://github.com/lentago/osmunda/issues) |
| This guide | [lentago/lupinus](https://github.com/lentago/lupinus/issues) |

**What to include:** the step you were on, the exact command, the full output,
and your platform. If a runbook was wrong, unclear, or missing something, say
which line — that is the highest-value issue anyone can file here, and it is how
these documents get better.

## What is and is not promised

These are public, MIT-licensed repositories maintained by a small practice. Our
help is pro bono — free for nonprofits and volunteer-run organizations. It's
still best effort, though: no service-level agreement (no promise of how fast we
answer), no on-call. If you need a response time you can count on, that's an
engagement, and it's a
[fireable retainer](https://github.com/lentago/.github/blob/main/docs/adr/0007-client-owned-delivery-no-multi-tenant-saas.md)
rather than a subscription to something we run for you.

Nothing you adopt depends on us being reachable. That is deliberate and it is
the point: your systems run in your accounts, and if we vanished tomorrow you
would keep operating. What you would lose is help improving things, not the
things themselves.

## Handover

If the person who set all this up leaves, someone should be able to pick it up
from this repository alone. That's worth testing before you need it: hand the
repository to someone who didn't build it and ask them to find out what you run,
what it costs, and who to call. Whatever they can't answer is the gap to fill
next.
